# Launch posts for the new storefront (Canva brief)

For announcing the V12.3 look on Instagram and Facebook. Build these in Canva
from the store's own pictures only. Every claim below is already on the site;
nothing here invents reviews, customer counts or results.

## Pictures to use (all from the theme or the store's files)

| Picture | Where it lives | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest hero (trail, light through the trees) | Theme > hero-film desktop and phone images | Background for the story and post 1 |
| GODA on the basalt ledge | `zorev-theme/assets/zorev-stage-amber.jpg` + the GODA cutout | Post 2 |
| HOYGI on the basalt ledge | `zorev-theme/assets/zorev-stage-sage.jpg` + the HOYGI cutout | Post 2 |
| The Pair photograph | Pair section image in `templates/index.json` | Post 3 |

Do not redraw or retype the product labels; place the real cutouts.

## Type and colour (match the site)

- Headings: Newsreader, regular weight (`zorev-theme/assets/newsreader-latin-400.woff2`).
- Small labels and buttons: Public Sans, 600, uppercase, letter-spaced.
- Ink `#101310`, warm white `#F4F2EC`, forest `#26372C`, sand `#D7D0C3` (from `zorev-tokens.css`).

## The three posts (feed 1080x1350, story 1080x1920)

1. **The new look**: forest hero full bleed. Headline "Made for wherever
   you’re headed." Line under it: "A 15 ml perfume oil and a 9 g balm stick."
   Button-style label: "SHOP ZOREV". Link: zorev.org.
2. **Two essentials**: GODA and HOYGI side by side on their ledges. Labels
   "PERFUME OIL" / "BALM STICK" and "GODA" / "HOYGI". Prices "$30" and "$25".
3. **The Pair**: the Pair photograph. Headline "GODA + HOYGI". Line "$47
   together. The saving is applied in your bag, no code."

Story version: post 1 at 9:16 with the headline in the top third and the
label clear of the bottom 250 px (where reply bars sit).

## Check before posting

- Prices match Shopify on the day (the site reads them live; these are
  $30 / $25 / Pair $47 as of 2026-10-02).
- Only say "out within one business day" if orders really do ship that fast.
