ZOREV carries two things. A 15 ml perfume oil in a glass dropper bottle, and a 9 g balm stick that goes on dry. That is the whole shop.

Why only two

A short list is easier to be honest about. We can tell you what is in each box, what it weighs, who made it and what the maker says about it — and where the maker's claims stop and ours begin. Forty products would make that impossible.

What we make, and what we don't

We do not manufacture anything. The perfume oil is made by GODA and the balm stick by Hoygi, both manufactured in China, and we say so on each product page. We choose the products, we write the listings, and we answer for them if something goes wrong. The photography on the product pages comes from the makers. Anyone who tells you otherwise about a shop this size is selling you a story.

About the claims on these products

Both makers market their products with claims we cannot verify — plumping and line care on the balm, pheromones on the oil. We repeat those as the manufacturer's claims and label them that way, and we do not build our own copy on top of them. What we will speak to is what the things physically are: a moisturising balm in stick form, and a perfume oil worn from a dropper.

How orders reach you

Shipping is free on every order, worldwide, with no minimum and no paid upgrade. Orders leave through our fulfilment network within one business day. Transit times, customs and returns are covered on the Shipping & Delivery page.

Reviews

ZOREV is new. Nobody has reviewed an order from this store yet, and where the site has room for reviews it says exactly that rather than filling itself with words we wrote ourselves. When real reviews come in, they will be from real orders.
