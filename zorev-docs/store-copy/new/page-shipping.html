What it costs

Nothing. Shipping is free on every order — no minimum, no paid upgrade. One rate, and it is zero.

Where we ship

To 189 countries, including the US, UK, the EU, Canada and Australia, every order with tracking. If your country isn't offered at checkout, we can't deliver there yet.

How long it takes

Typically 8 to 12 business days from order to doorstep. Your order goes to the fulfilment network within one business day of being placed, and the rest is transit and customs.

Those are estimates based on how these routes normally run — not guarantees. Carrier delays, weather and customs inspections are outside our control, and we would rather give you an honest range than a number we cannot hold.

Tracking

You get a tracking number by email as soon as the carrier scans the parcel. If the tracking has not moved in five business days, email us and we will chase it.

Customs and import duty

Depending on your country and where your parcel ships from, import duty or tax may be charged on delivery. It is collected by customs or the carrier, not by us, it isn't included in the price here, and we can't quote it in advance.

If it does not arrive

If a parcel is lost in transit or the tracking dies, we replace it or refund it — your choice. Email zorevhousing@gmail.com with your order number.

Returns

Returns follow our refund policy, which excludes personal care goods — and both ZOREV products are personal care goods, so we cannot take them back for a change of mind. If an order arrives damaged, defective or wrong, email us with your order number and a photo and we will look into it and make it right. Orders shipped into the European Union can be cancelled or returned within 14 days of delivery, unused and in the original packaging.
