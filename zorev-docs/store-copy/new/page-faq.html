How long does delivery take?

Typically 8 to 12 business days. Orders go to our fulfilment network within one business day, and you get a tracking number by email as soon as the carrier scans the parcel. Those dates are estimates, not guarantees — carrier handling is not something we control.

What does shipping cost?

Nothing. Free on every order to the 189 countries we deliver to, with tracking. There is no minimum and no paid upgrade.

Will I owe customs or import duty?

Possibly. Depending on your country and where your parcel ships from, import duty or tax may be charged on delivery. It is collected by customs or the carrier, not by us, it isn't included in the price here, and we can't quote it in advance.

It is a moisturising balm in stick form, and Hoygi lists it for plumping, line care, age-defying and moisturising — those are their claims, not ours, and we are not going to dress them up.
