Returns
GODA and HOYGI are personal care goods, so we can't accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind. Not sure a product is right for you? Email zorevhousing@gmail.com before you buy and a person will answer.

Damaged, defective or wrong items
Please check your order when it arrives. If anything is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, email zorevhousing@gmail.com with your order number and a photo, and we'll make it right.

European Union: 14-day cooling-off period
If your order ships to the European Union, you can cancel it or return it within 14 days of delivery, for any reason, as long as it is unused and in its original packaging. Email us first and we'll send you the return details; parcels sent back without an approved return can't be accepted.

Refunds
Once a refund is approved, it goes back to your original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank or card company may take a little longer to post it. If more than 15 business days have passed since we approved your refund, email zorevhousing@gmail.com.
